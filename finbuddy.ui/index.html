Automatic UPI capture

On Android, FinBuddy can look for payment notifications from Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm and BHIM. Android will ask you to grant notification access. Matching is done on this device; notification text can contain sensitive details. Capture may miss payments when an app does not show a readable notification, and you can turn access off in Android Settings.

Android app required

This browser page cannot read notifications from other apps.
